Entangled Holds

Knot-theoretic refinement of the hand-partition dance census from Maril's “A New Kind of Dance Science”. Arms are tangle strands, torsos are obstacle bars, grips fuse hand endpoints.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Over/under crossings in the 3D tubes and the gaps in the 2D diagram both come from the same braid-word data. Invariants are computed live in the browser with exact (bigint) arithmetic.

3D — facing stance, torso bars as obstacles
2D — tangle diagram (braid-style, shoulders at bottom)
